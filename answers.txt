Agile Pros
Flexible and adapts easily to changing requirements.
Delivers working features early and continuously.
Agile cons
Harder to predict cost, scope, and timelines.
Requires constant collaboration and stakeholder input.

Scrum Master vs Product owner

Scrum Master:
- Facilitates Scrum process and removes blockers.
- Leads meetings and supports team improvement.

Product Owner:
- Manages product backlog and priorities.
- Defines vision and communicates with stakeholders.

MeetingTypes

1. Sprint Planning
   - Define sprint goal and select backlog items.
   - Break down tasks and estimate effort.

2. Daily Stand-up
   - Share what was done yesterday and plan for today.
   - Discuss blockers or obstacles.

3. Sprint Review
   - Demonstrate completed features to stakeholders.
   - Collect feedback to update backlog.


IssueTypes

Epic:
- Large body of work, big feature or initiative.
- Broken down into multiple stories/tasks.

Story:
- End-user functionality or requirement.
- Delivers value to the customer.

Bug:
- Error or defect in the product.
- Needs fixing to restore expected behavior.

Task:
- General work item (not always user-facing).
- Supports stories, bugs, or epics.
